# The World of Tennis 🎾

Tennis is a sport that combines physical fitness, concentration, strategy, and discipline. It can be played individually or with a partner in doubles. Every match is an opportunity to improve your skills and challenge yourself.

## Why I Enjoy Playing Tennis

Tennis is more than just hitting a ball over a net. It teaches patience, builds confidence, and improves decision-making.

- It improves physical fitness and stamina.
- It develops hand-eye coordination.
- It teaches discipline and sportsmanship.
- It helps develop mental strength and concentration.

**Consistency is one of the most important qualities in tennis.**

*Every point is a new opportunity to improve.*

***Hard work and practice make a difference on the court.***

### Basic Equipment Needed

To play tennis, you need a few essential items:

- Tennis racket
- Tennis balls
- Comfortable sports shoes
- Appropriate sports clothing
- Water bottle

## How to Prepare for a Tennis Match

Here are five simple steps I follow before playing:

1. Warm up with light jogging.
2. Stretch the arms, shoulders, and legs.
3. Practise a few groundstrokes.
4. Hit some serves to find your rhythm.
5. Get mentally ready for the match.

## A Simple Tennis Practice Example

A tennis player can follow a simple practice routine. For example, `practice("forehand", 20)` represents practising twenty forehand shots.

Here is a small Python example of a practice counter:

```python
shots = 20

for shot in range(1, shots + 1):
    print("Practising shot:", shot)

print("Practice completed!")
```
